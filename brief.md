# CampusEats brief

## What

CampusEats is an online food ordering platform built for a college campus. 
Users can explore food offerings from various campus vendors, check
product details and pricing, add items to a shopping cart, and place
orders online. The platform also lets users monitor the status of their orders.

## Who

The primary users of CampusEats include:

- Students
- Faculty and staff
- Food vendors
- Administrators

Students, faculty, and staff buy food through the platform. Vendors
post their food offerings and handle incoming orders. Administrators
oversee users, vendors, products, and the platform as a whole.

## Summary

CampusEats is an online food ordering platform built for a college
campus. Users can find food items from different campus vendors,
look through menus, add items to their cart, place orders, complete
payments, and monitor their food orders. Vendors can manage their
food listings and incoming orders, while administrators oversee the
platform as a whole.
